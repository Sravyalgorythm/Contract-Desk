# Sample Contract Test Guide

Upload `sample_contract.docx` in Contract Desk. It is entirely fictional, made
only to exercise document extraction, retrieval, citations, and abstention. Do
not sign it, use it for a transaction, or treat it as legal advice.

## Questions to try

- **Who are the parties, and which one is the Customer?** Expect Redwood Sample
  Systems LLC as Customer and Harborlight Test Services, Inc. as Provider. Check
  the opening paragraph citation.
- **What is the monthly subscription fee and when is payment due?** Expect $1,200
  per month, due within 30 days after receipt of an undisputed invoice.
- **How long is the initial term, and how does renewal work?** Expect one year,
  followed by automatic one-year renewals unless either Party gives 60 days'
  written non-renewal notice.
- **What is the service availability commitment and credit?** Expect 99.5% monthly
  availability and a 10% credit for an eligible shortfall, subject to the stated
  exclusions and request deadline.
- **What law governs the agreement?** Expect Washington State law and courts in
  King County, Washington.
- **Does the contract require the Provider to carry $5 million in cyber insurance?**
  That requirement is deliberately absent. The assistant should not claim that
  it is required; inspect the retrieved evidence and answer.

For each supported answer, inspect the displayed DOCX heading/paragraph or table
location and confirm it supports the statement. If the parties question does not
show the opening paragraph under Evidence, lower the relevance threshold and try
again; if evidence includes the party names but the answer is withheld, check that
the model cited an `[Evidence N]` label.
